About us

Shreeji Girls PG is a girls-only paying guest accommodation in Thaltej, Ahmedabad. We host students and working women who want a safe place to live with the comforts of home.

  • Girls-only building with CCTV and a caretaker
  • Furnished rooms with cupboards and study tables
  • Home-style vegetarian meals
  • Wi-Fi, laundry, RO water and power backup
  • Easy access to SG Highway, colleges and offices

Rules and policies

  1. Girls only. Male visitors are not allowed inside rooms or the building.
  2. Gate timing. The gate closes at 10:00 PM. Please inform the caretaker in advance if you will be late.
  3. Rent. Pay rent by the 5th of every month. Late payment may attract a fee.
  4. Security deposit. Refundable at check-out after room inspection, within 15 days.
  5. Notice period. Give 30 days' notice before leaving. Otherwise one month's rent is deducted.
  6. No smoking, alcohol or drugs on the premises.
  7. Cleanliness. Keep your room and common areas tidy. Switch off lights, fans and AC when not in use.
  8. Property. Damage to furniture or fittings will be charged to the resident.
  9. Valuables. Residents are responsible for their own belongings.
  10. Respect. Keep noise low after 10:00 PM and treat everyone with courtesy.
